1. Who we are and what esHoy is

esHoy is an app for running your business from your phone: sales, payments, appointments, loans and properties. It is developed and supported by APD-BIS (bumapp.cloud).

By installing or using the app you accept these terms. If you do not agree, uninstall it.

2. Your account and your contact information

To use esHoy you must enter your business name, a WhatsApp number and an email address. They must be yours and up to date, because that is how we give you support and let you know about important changes.

You can correct this information at any time in Settings → My business.

3. What data we store and what we do not

Your sales, customers, products, expenses, loans and the rest of your business information are stored on your phone. We do not send them to our servers.

We do store on our servers: your business name, your WhatsApp number, your email, the sections you use, the app version and the installation and last-use dates. We use them for support, to count how many businesses use esHoy and to improve the app.

If you turn on online bookings, your hours, services and the bookings made by your customers (their name and phone number) are also stored on our servers for a limited time.

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. To ask us to delete yours, write to us through bumapp.cloud.

4. Backing up your information

Because your data lives on your phone, if you lose it, it breaks, or you uninstall the app without a backup, you could lose it. esHoy saves an automatic copy every day in the Documents/esHoy/auto folder of your phone, but if you lose the phone that copy is lost too. If your phone has Android backup turned on, the system may also save a copy of the app's data in your Google account; Google manages that, not us. Make your backup from Settings → Data backup often and keep it outside the phone. You are responsible for keeping it.

5. Online bookings

You are responsible for serving the bookings you receive, keeping your hours up to date and your relationship with your customers. The property prices shown to the customer are an estimate; you confirm and charge.
