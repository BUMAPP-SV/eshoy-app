Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

esHoy is an app for keeping track of a business's sales, finances and bookings. This page explains what information it uses and how it is handled. It is short on purpose.

1. What is stored and where

  • On your phone: products, sales, expenses, income, customers, credit, appointments and your business settings (including the language and currency you choose). This information does not leave your device, except when you save or share a backup, a receipt or a report.
  • On a server, as an installation record: while you use the app, the following is sent periodically (at most once a day): the name of your business, the WhatsApp number and email you entered, a random installation identifier, the sections you have turned on (for example sales, barbershop, nails, properties, loans or online bookings; names only), the app version, the platform, and the installation and last-use dates. Your sales, customers, products and expenses are not sent.
  • On a server, only if you turn on online bookings: the name and phone number of the person booking, the service or property chosen and the date. Also the hours, services, prices, currency and language you publish so your customers can book.

What we use the installation record for

  • To support you and to contact you about the app and its improvements.
  • To count how many businesses use esHoy and which sections are used, so we can improve it.
  • We keep this record while the app remains in use and delete it after 365 days without activity or when you ask us to.

2. Online bookings

  • The information for each booking is kept for up to 90 days after the appointment or stay ends, and is then deleted automatically.
  • Only the business that owns the link can see the bookings it receives. They are not sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • The public booking page uses an anti-spam check (Cloudflare Turnstile) to prevent automated submissions.

3. Notifications

If you accept notifications, the phone registers a technical identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging, from Google) to alert you about new bookings. The alert does not include your customer's phone number. You can turn them off at any time from Settings or from Android.

Who processes the information

The server runs on Cloudflare and notifications run on Firebase (Google). They act as providers that process data on our behalf; everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). The app is developed and supported by APD-BIS (bumapp.cloud).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell information or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do not access your contacts or your location.
  • It is not aimed at minors: it is a tool for businesses.

5. App permissions

  • Internet: for online bookings, their alerts, the installation record and update checks.
  • Notifications: to alert you about new bookings.
  • Camera: only if you scan barcodes or add a product photo.
  • Bluetooth: only if you connect a thermal printer.

6. Your choices

  • Data on your phone: delete it by uninstalling the app or from Settings.
  • Installation record: if you remove your WhatsApp or email in Settings → My business, it is also deleted from the server. You can ask for your whole record, and your business's booking data, to be deleted by writing to us through bumapp.cloud.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with its new date.
