How to install

Ready in under a minute.

esHoy is downloaded as an APK file. Android will ask for a permission the first time; that is normal.

Download

Tap "Download free APK" from your Android phone.

Open it

When it finishes, tap the esHoy.apk file in your notifications or in Downloads.

Allow the install

If Android warns you, turn on "Allow from this source" and go back.

Install and open

Tap Install. When you open esHoy, choose your language, enter your business name and start selling.

If Play Protect shows an "unverified app" warning, you can choose Install anyway: the app is not on Play Store yet.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us the most.

Is it really free?

Yes. Downloading it, using it and receiving bookings costs nothing: no subscription, no credit card and no ads.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Selling, charging and tracking expenses and customer credit work offline; everything is saved on your phone. Only online bookings and their alerts need internet.

Which languages and currencies does it support?

The app and the online booking page work in English and Spanish. Each business uses one currency, chosen from 18 (USD, EUR, MXN, GTQ, HNL, NIO, CRC, PAB, DOP, COP, PEN, ARS, BOB, UYU, VES, BRL, GBP and CAD). Changing the currency later does not convert past amounts, it only changes how they are written. Phone numbers from any country are accepted.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone. The only things that travel over the internet are what online bookings need (the name and phone of the person booking) and a basic contact record for your business (name, WhatsApp and email) so we can support you; never your sales or your customers. You can save a backup whenever you want. Read the privacy policy.

Why does Android warn me this is an unknown app?

Because it is installed from an APK file and not from Play Store. It is the standard Android warning for any app that does not come from the store. Only install APKs downloaded from this site.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now it is only for Android 7.0 or later.

What happens if I change phones?

Save a backup from Settings → Data backup and restore it on the new phone. For online bookings you will need your recovery code.

Can I issue electronic invoices (DTE)?

esHoy issues simple tickets and receipts; it does not include electronic invoicing. If your business needs it, see bumapp.
